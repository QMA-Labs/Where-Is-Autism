# From proposition to a first test

[← Project](../README.md) · [Framework](THEORY.md)

## One primary question

Do prespecified context and response-interface measurements improve prediction of a defined task outcome on held-out participants beyond a person-only baseline?

**Candidate hypothesis:** added variables provide reproducible incremental prediction. **Null comparison:** improvement is absent or below a prespecified meaningful margin. Neither outcome alone establishes causality.

## Decisions before data collection

1. Work with autistic contributors to select an accessible, meaningful outcome and acceptable conditions.
2. Define the outcome, baseline and added variables. Examine construct validity, task difficulty, motor and language demands.
3. Set the minimum useful improvement and analysis plan before seeing outcomes. Determine sample size through an appropriate power or simulation study.
4. Obtain suitable ethics approval and accessible consent/assent before recruitment. Preserve choice, communication preferences and the ability to stop.
5. Prespecify handling of practice, fatigue, order, context and missingness. Keep each person and family within one evaluation split.

## A staged path

| Stage | Deliverable | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scientific gap | Critique of overlap with existing work; revised source map | Is there a distinct, useful question? |
| Measurement feasibility | Matched tasks and accessible reporting | Are measures suitable and reliable? |
| Prespecified comparison | Baseline, enriched predictor, outcome and decision margin | Does the added information help on held-out observations? |
| Independent evaluation | New cohort or setting, uncertainty and sensitivity analyses | Does it generalize? |

## What would change our minds?

Negligible improvement; instrument dependence; disappearance under appropriate adjustment; unstable representations; or failure to replicate. Retain negative results. Do not redefine success after inspecting outcomes.

## Later questions are separate projects

Measured family traits require shared-environment and reporting considerations. A regression interaction is not information-theoretic synergy. Feature-space discovery requires reliable features, scaling checks, stability and independent evaluation. Sensory questions need specific dimensions and participant-selected outcomes, rather than one universal load score.

## Useful feedback now

Suggest a stronger baseline, a better existing explanation, an unsuitable instrument, missing contradictory evidence or a more tractable comparison. This is a request for critique and measurement guidance, without assumed affiliation, recruitment, funding or endorsement.

This agenda is for discussion. No completed study, preregistration or empirical result is claimed.
