# The visual argument

The cover shows a field of relationships: ivory and pale-blue filaments, sparse amber paths and an open center. It is an editorial metaphor, not participant data or neuroanatomy.

Warm serif typography references a research monograph. Fine rules and restrained labels connect it to the application’s dark interface. Actual application captures show implemented behavior separately.
