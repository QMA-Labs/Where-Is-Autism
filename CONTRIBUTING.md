# Contribute a sharper question

A useful contribution makes a claim easier to inspect, test or revise.

For research feedback, identify the [claim](docs/CLAIM-ATLAS.md), explain the inference you question, link relevant evidence where possible and suggest a rival explanation or distinguishing test. Corrections and contradictory evidence are welcome. Use the [research-question template](https://github.com/QMA-Labs/Where-Is-Autism/issues/new?template=research-question.yml).

For software changes, describe the user-visible problem, keep content separate from presentation and validate the affected portable build. Follow [development instructions](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md). Propose external runtime calls explicitly; the portable editions currently work offline.

Keep illustrative values identified as synthetic. Avoid unverified diagnosis, accuracy, treatment or endorsement claims. Do not post identifiable participant records or private medical information.

No project-wide license has been selected. Third-party notices remain with their respective components; do not assume a new license for text, software or artwork.
