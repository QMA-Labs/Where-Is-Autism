# WHERE IS AUTISM? — design contract v1

Locked before implementation. An observatory for questions, not a diagnostic dashboard.

## Visual system
Near-black #07090d; surfaces #10151d; primary text #f1f4f8; secondary #a6b0c0; ice #a4d9ee; violet #a6a5e6. Thin borders, restrained translucent surfaces, no neon rainbow. Sans-serif editorial headings with tight tracking; monospace coordinates and metadata. Main text 16–18px. Spacious 8px rhythm. A generative, non-anatomical geometry is the central visual: a metaphor, never a brain scan.

Motion: 500ms chapter entrances, subtle pointer parallax, slow geometry rotation. Reduced-motion preference disables automatic animation and parallax. All paths remain keyboard accessible; visible focus, labelled controls, no autoplay sound or forced timed reading.

## Information architecture
Initialization → core question → research premise → four pillars → Masiha → spatial atlas.
The atlas has eight chapters: Family, Hidden Mind, State, Environment, Mathematics, AI Discovery, Evidence, Challenge the Theory.
Each contains a visual metaphor, simple/scientific explanation, interactive synthetic visualization, evidence and limitations, and report summary. Shared navigation offers Introduction, Atlas, Evidence, and the curated guide. Simple and Scientific modes change explanation depth.

## Research integrity
The distributed-mind framing is a hypothesis. No study dataset or manuscript was provided. Generated values are explicitly synthetic, not estimates. No diagnoses, severity scores, clinical predictions, or claims of novelty. Mathematical examples describe toy systems; no invented family synergy estimator. Evidence is separated into background literature and unvalidated project claims.

## Curated guide
Deterministic question bank routed to the current chapter. Layered simple, scientific, math, evidence and limits answers; objection questions; keyword filtering with a transparent no-match state. No language model, remote prompt or fabricated response. Future data adapters must provide provenance, units, consent/governance status and validation metadata before replacing demo data.
